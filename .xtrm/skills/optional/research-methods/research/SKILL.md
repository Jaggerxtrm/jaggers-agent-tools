# Research

Research is evidence acquisition, not a license to browse everything.

| Need | Read |
|---|---|
| Current library/SDK/CLI docs | `references/documentation.md` |
| Real public implementation examples | `references/code-search.md` |
| Recent ecosystem/community/news evidence | `references/recent-web.md` |
| Multi-source/academic investigation | `references/deep-research.md` |
| Verify a concrete claim | `references/fact-checking.md` |

Prefer primary/current sources for specifications and APIs. Community sources are useful
for operational experience and emerging practice, but separate them from authoritative
claims.

Do not copy third-party research skills that require broad credentials/browser cookies
into the default XTRM surface. Add such integrations deliberately to a user/domain pack
when their value justifies the trust boundary.
