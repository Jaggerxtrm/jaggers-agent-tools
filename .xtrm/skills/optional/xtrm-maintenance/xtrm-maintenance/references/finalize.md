# Finalize and handoff

Use the current `xt` integration/end/report surfaces when closing XTRM-maintained work.
The stable requirements are: reconcile open work, verify the final diff and checks,
persist handoff/evidence, publish through the repository's current PR/merge policy, and
clean disposable worktrees/processes only after proving they are no longer needed.

Do not auto-resolve complex conflicts or destructive cleanup merely because an old
session-close skill did so.
